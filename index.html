Let It Be

Major Key Identification Ear Trainer

In this game you will be played a short recording of an arrangement of The Beatles' track 'Let It Be'. You will need to use your keyboard / piano to audition notes / major scales to find the key that the recording is in. On the next page you can select which Major Keys to include in the game.

Hint! Playing the major scale of the key the recording is in should sound complimentary / pleasant. Ie if the recording is in C Major, playing the C Major scale over the top of the recording should sound nice. Playing other Major scales will sound less nice, and possibly quite unpleasant! Ie if the recording is in C Major, playing the A Major scale on your piano at the same time as playing the recording in the game could sound - subjectively - quite unpleasant!
